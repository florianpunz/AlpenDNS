## Critical Anti-Patterns (do NOT repeat these)

- **Claiming a neutral page without the right sentence.** A detector-blocked
  name reaches the neutral branch because `Effects::ReadOnly` skips detectors.
  The page may say "No blocklist entry applies to this name."; it must not say
  the name was allowed or unblocked.
- **Using `policy::explain` for display.** It counts and it runs detectors.
  Display paths use `block_cause`.
